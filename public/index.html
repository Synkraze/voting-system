THE VOTING PROCESS

How does online voting work?

  1. 01

    Student Authentication

    Enter your registered university email address to verify your eligibility.

  2. 02

    Email OTP Verification

    A One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered university email address.

  3. 03

    View Candidates

    Review the candidates contesting in your respective level.

  4. 04

    Cast Your Vote

    Select your preferred candidates for the positions you choose, then confirm before submission.

  5. 05

    Vote Confirmation

    Once submitted, your vote will be securely recorded. You cannot vote again.

YOUR BALLOT MATTERS

Security & Transparency

Secure Student Authentication

Only eligible students can access the voting process through identity verification and OTP authentication.

Administrator Ballot Review

Authorized election administrators can link voter records to ballot choices. If a voter is removed for a recorded reason, their linked ballots are excluded from the tally while remaining in the audit chain.

Verifiable Voting Records

After the election closes, ballot records can be independently verified as part of the election transparency process.

VOTE RESPONSIBLY

Important Voting Guidelines

  • Each eligible student is entitled to one vote.
  • Do not share your OTP with anyone.
  • Review your selection before submitting.
  • Votes cannot be modified after final submission.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of your voting credentials.
